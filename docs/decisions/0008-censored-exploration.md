## Decision

The routing engine learns under **bandit (per-arm) feedback as the standing feedback scope**, with three scoped protocols on top:

1. **Exploration strategy** — Thompson sampling's probability matching *is* the exploration mechanism. Its measured annealing (4.3% → 1.8% explorative picks per 10k decisions on the steady world) is a feature on stationary worlds and the censorship trap on moved ones; the responder to motion is the ADR-0007 detector, never a standing mechanism. On detector reset, the tiered decay applies to **both data counts and prior pseudo-counts** (the m=100 prior, not the ~3 data counts, is what binds a written-off fine arm); a re-firing alarm while an arm is still in its reset-onboarding state **compounds the decay but does not restart the R49 onboarding clock**.
2. **Forced-exploration mechanism** — **none standing**. ε-greedy overlays and quota rotation are rejected with their billed taxes (−79/−467/−519 c/1k steady-margin at ε=0.01/0.05/ρ=0.02 on `baseline-steady-v1`, 60k txns); optimistic initialization is rejected as inert (−29 c/1k, and a prior is not a mechanism). The only forced exploration in the system remains ADR-0006's R49 onboarding floor (η=0.05, n_min=1000), which fires on processor onboarding and on detector-triggered resets — a *targeted* budget spent on exactly the arm that changed, only while it is re-learning. Its measured role: on improvement events the *reset* (data&prior decay) does the refeeding and the floor adds ~1pt of share there ([C2] boundary case); the floor's necessity case is ADR-0007's measured degradation re-entry; treating it as metered event-insurance, not a standing engine, is the balance this decision strikes.
3. **Propensity logging schema** — **DECISION_LOG v1**: per decision, {seq, arrival_ms, context key (bin, region, sca, mandate, amount, band), eligible-set bitmap, per-eligible-arm posterior params (α, β, τ_a, τ_b), chain, score-based plug-in propensity with method tag, floor state}; per run, a header {policy_seed, hashes of arm-schema/catalog/constraint-set/prior-artifact, λ_to}. Draws are *not* logged — they are key-addressed pure functions of (policy_seed, seq, arm, purpose) given the posterior and re-derive bit-exactly. The logged propensity is **score-based** (P(arm's score argmax), score = θ·win − (1−θ)·fee − π·λ_to), not θ-only: the θ-plug-in is systematically biased on a margin-skewed fleet (measured 83.6 pts mean chosen-arm error vs a 100k-round score MC reference; the score-based plug-in cuts that to 14.1 pts chosen-arm mean — still a logged approximation; #15 recomputes exact propensities, where MC-64 already reaches 0.8 pts).
4. **Delayed-outcome handling protocol** — **update on arrival, never on a wait**: a settled outcome (webhook → queue → learner) enters the posterior when it arrives, in WAL order; no holding window. The terminal default at the derived deadline (ADR-0001 R4) is **TIMEOUT, excluded from the auth posterior and priced at λ_to** — **never** treated as a decline. A late-settling outcome (issuer answers after the deadline) **never rewrites the auth label**: it increments the late counter (feeding reconciliation and λ_to recalibration) and is otherwise observability. Ingest lag is measured to be free up to 256 txns (~45 min at fleet pace) and degrades geometrically after (−115 c/1k at 8,192 txns), which sets lag-budget expectations for #13.

## Context

ADR-0002 fixed the estimand — the learner targets P(authorized | attempt, not timeout), with timeouts excluded (R10) and priced (R12) — and ADR-0003 chose probability matching over ε-greedy and UCB. Neither priced *censoring itself*, and the ticket that tracks it (#9) demands four concrete protocols this ADR pins. The question "does Thompson sampling's built-in exploration suffice?" had only partial answers: ADR-0006 showed the floor repairs cold start; ADR-0007 showed the detector repairs degradation. The improvement direction — an arm you wrote off getting *good* — is newly measured here: it is the only direction in which the router's feedback is genuinely filtered by its own routing decisions, because a degradation announces itself on the traffic you *do* send while an improvement only shows on the traffic you *don't*. The spike adds `quiet-improvement-v1` (competitor believed 0.806, silently becomes 0.92, no transport signature) and a starved variant (the prior artifact refreshed during the storm, so the arm enters the event at ~2.9% share) to make the trap concrete.

### What was measured (60,000 decisions per run unless noted)

- **C1, the price of censoring.** Bandit feedback vs a full-information learner folding all eligible arms' counterfactuals each decision (impossible in production; free in the harness): steady world 27,376.2 → 27,468.8 c/1k (+92.6) and MAE 4.25 → 4.06 pts; outage world +92.0 c/1k, −0.19 pts. Censoring costs precision and speed, not correctness — bandit labels are unbiased by construction (the world answers an attempt without knowing whether it was chosen or probed; missing-at-random conditional on the policy).
- **C2, built-in exploration.** Steady-world explorative-pick share anneals 4.31% → 1.85% over 60k decisions. On `quiet-improvement-v1` (event at seq 25,952; argmax flips in 6.1% of contexts; oracle headroom 1,312 c/1k): bare TS feeds foxtrot at 10.5% globally pre and post forever; the ADR-0007 machinery (ADWIN δ=1e-3 + decay + floor) detects the improvement with **zero false positives** after 6,204 txns / 65,286s of post-event feed, lifting flip-context foxtrot share 17.6% → 25.2% (oracle 49.1%) and global share to 13.6%. Decaying data-only moves the share less (→12.6%) than decaying data+prior (→13.6%) — the prior is the binding pseudo-count. First alarm is tier-2 (|Δμ|=0.099, te=0).
- **C2 boundary case.** In the starved variant (prior believes 0.60; pre-share 2.93%): bare TS never detects (no alarm; 6.5% flip-share; the arm anneals away at 3%). The detector variants see the 0.60→0.92 step after **4,581 txns / 35,350s** of trickle feed — *faster* than the milder 0.806→0.92 step through the main case's 10.6% feed (6,204 txns), because for the statistic, contrast dominates feed rate. The data&prior-decay reset then re-feeds the arm to ~12% global / ~27% flip-share against bare's 3.6% / 6.5%; the R49 floor adds only ~+1pt over decay-only here — in this configuration the reset, not the floor, refeeds.
- **C3, standing mechanisms.** Steady-world per-day-of-every-week tax vs bare TS (27,376.2 c/1k): ε-greedy 0.01 −79.0, ε-greedy 0.05 −466.8 (1,535 forced attempts), rotation ρ=0.02 per-2k −519.1 (1,168 forced), optimistic prior ×1.08 −28.8. On the improvement world none of them beats bare TS's full-run margin; the detector machinery's current R49-form nets below bare at the 4-day window while carrying positive rescue share. Optimistic init's effect washes out after ~25k txns of data.
- **C4, the decision log.** Decision replay from logged posteriors + key-addressed draws: 426/426 bit-exact. WAL prefix-fold == decision-time posterior snapshot: exact match. Propensities vs score-based MC(R=100,000) over 48 logged states, mean chosen-arm |Δp|: θ-only plug-in **83.6 pts** (systematically broken on this margin-skewed fleet); score-based plug-in **14.1 pts** worst-state; MC-64 0.8 pts (any-arm max 7.7). Record cost: 104 B/decision fixed-layout (f32 posteriors, k_max=5) ≈ 44.9 GB/day at 5,000 dps; 184 B f64. Eligibility after the constraint filter is nontrivial in 87.4% of decisions; the full fleet is never eligible (0.0%).
- **C5, delayed outcomes.** Ingest lag on the steady world: lag 0 27,376.2; lag 64 ≈ +7; lag 256 ≈ +8; lag 1,024 −27; lag 8,192 −115 c/1k and +0.39 MAE pts. Lag × drift-reset on the outage world: the first alarm moves 43,424 → 47,566 as lag grows 0 → 4,096 (the reset just lands later; the machinery is lag-tolerant). Late-settlement relabeling (~676 late arrivals consumed in-window): shipped exclusion vs retro-relabel vs no-response=decline: MAE **4.25 / 4.24 / 4.68 pts**; margins 27,376.2 / 27,330.1 / 27,363.4 — both alternatives *lose* margin (−46/−13), inside path noise, so the verdict is estimand-lead: retro-relabel's labels are marginally less biased (a wash), and on ADR-0002's ~10%-timeout trace the decline protocol measured 26,646 vs 27,650 c/1k for exclusion (**−1,004 c/1k**). Estimand stands; timeout share is the boundary.

### Considerations addressed

1. **Censorship precisely**: the estimand is per-arm; labels are MAR conditional on the policy, so the posterior is unbiased but slow; the only genuine censorship trap is a *moved truth* on a starved arm — which is why the mechanism is detector-triggered, not standing. Survivorship bias in the "stale posterior" sense is a variance statement, not a bias statement (C1).
2. **Built-in exploration sufficiency**: sufficient where the world is stationary (by design — annealing), sufficient to *detect* moves even at ~3% share (4,581 txns refractory lag on the starved arm — and the bigger the move, the thinner the feed it needs), insufficient to *re-learn* them at fleet pace within days without a responder (C2).
3. **Forced exploration**: no standing overlay; R49 remains the only forced exploration, now scoped: decay applies to data *and* prior pseudo-counts, and re-fire during onboarding doesn't re-arm the floor clock (C3, C2).
4. **Logging for OPE**: DECISION_LOG v1 with the score-based plug-in propensity tagged; replays bit-exact; #15 recomputes exact propensities via WAL replay (C4).
5. **Delayed outcomes**: update-on-arrival; deadline-terminal is timeout-priced not decline-flavored; late settlements never relabel (C5).

## Alternatives considered

- **Full-information learning** (update all arms from one attempt's outcomes): measured bound, +92.6 c/1k / −0.19 MAE pts — the theoretical price of censoring on this fleet, and *physically unavailable*: we cannot ask Adyen what it would have done with traffic sent to Stripe.
- **θ-only plug-in propensity** (ADR-0006 P4e form): rejected — 83.6 pts mean chosen-arm error on a margin-skewed fleet (delta's auth-strong-margin-thin profile is over-propensed against charlie's auth-weak-margin-fat profile); saved as a cautionary row in [C4]. The score-based plug-in keeps same cost class (14.1 pts chosen-arm mean; any-arm error can still reach 100 in pathological near-tie states, which is precisely why #15's exactness comes from WAL replay, not from the tag).
- **Standing ε-greedy / rotation quotas / optimistic initialization**: rejected with billed steady-world taxes (−79 to −519 / −29 c/1k) and no improvement-world win vs bare TS at the measured horizon.
- **Retroactive relabeling on late settlement**: rejected — moves the estimand from *authorized* to *eventually authorized*, i.e. evaluates arms as if the router had no deadline. Its label set is marginally *less* biased (MAE 4.24 vs 4.25 — a wash at this precision) and it even loses margin here (−46 c/1k, path noise); the protocol buys nothing the late counter doesn't already surface.
- **No-response = decline at the deadline**: rejected — corrupts the auth posterior (MAE +0.43 pts here; −1,004 c/1k on ADR-0002's timeout-heavy trace; −13 c/1k here), and the estimand cannot express "the merchant gave up".
- **Hold-the-update wait protocol** (wait for confirmation before folding an outcome): rejected — ingest lag is margin-neutral up to ~256 txns and harmful after; waiting buys certainty we never need because outcomes are terminal by definition once the issuer answers.
- **Keep decay data-only (ADR-0007 as shipped)**: superseded — on improvement events the m=100 prior is the binding pseudo-count (~100 vs ~3 data counts), so decaying data alone under-feeds the detector's reset; C2/C3 tables carry both rows for the record.

## Consequences

### Easier

- #12's `Decide` return value is now closed: it yields the DECISION_LOG v1 fields; nothing else needs to leave the hot path.
- #13 can size the log pipeline from measured bytes (104 B/decision f32; 184 B f64 — both quoted at 5,000 dps in [C4]).
- #15 can build IPS straight on the logged record (relies on replay PASS + WAL fold PASS) and recomputes exact propensities offline; the *tagged* plug-in is an approximation with a measured error budget (≤5.6 pts chosen-arm mean at this fleet/posterior range), so OPE uses the recompute path, not the tag, whenever exactness matters.
- #16 gets the panels implied by the rules below (late counter rate, ADWIN alarm ledger, floor clock state, exploration annealing).

### Harder

- The routing engine must carry score components (win/fee per decision) into the propensity estimator at log time — the score-based plug-in needs `win`, `fee`, `λ_to`, τ-posterior; it is the same cost class as the θ-plug-in (quadrature over the τ posterior).
- The detector reset must now scale prior pseudo-counts too, which means the prior-artifact contract (R50) grows a decay hook — implemented as an additional WAL record the fold understands; replay remains closed (C4 check 2).
- The starved boundary says prior *refresh* (#11) is now a routing-relevant decision: refreshing the artifact during a degradation permanently dimms the arm until an alarm. This is intended (beliefs should follow the world) but must be visible in #16.

### Design rules added

- **R58** — On any detector-triggered reset, the tiered decay (ADR-0007 R54: γ∈{0.1, 0.5}) applies to the arm's data counts **and its prior pseudo-counts** (α₀, β₀, τ_a₀, τ_b₀) alike; a re-firing alarm on an arm still inside its reset-onboarding state compounds the decay but does **not** restart the n_min onboarding clock.
- **R59** — No standing forced-exploration mechanism is permitted on the routing path: no ε-greedy overlay, no rotation quota, no optimistic-initialization bias. Forced exploration exists only as ADR-0006's R49 onboarding floor, scoped to onboarding and detector resets. (Amends R49's operating domain; R49's η=0.05, n_min=1000 unchanged.)
- **R60** — The decision log is the **DECISION_LOG v1** record: {seq, arrival_ms, context key, eligible-set bitmap, per-eligible posterior (α, β, τ_a, τ_b), chain, score-based plug-in propensity + method tag, floor state} with a run header of policy_seed and parameter hashes. Sampling draws are never logged; they re-derive key-addressed. Any field change is a schema version bump, not an edit.
- **R61** — The logged propensity is P(arm's score is argmax) with **score = θ·win − (1−θ)·fee − π·λ_to**, never P(arm's θ is max). Offline evaluation (#15) recomputes exact propensities by WAL replay; the logged plug-in is provenance + sanity, tagged with its method.
- **R62** — Settled outcomes fold into the posterior **on arrival** in WAL order; no holding window. An attempt that reaches its derived deadline with no response terminals as **TIMEOUT** (excluded from the auth posterior, priced at λ_to) — never as a decline, and never later rewritten into an auth or a decline; its late resolution increments the late counter only.

### Reopen triggers

- If the fleet's timeout share crosses ~6% sustained, re-run C5(c): the decline-protocol's penalty grows somewhere between the measured 4.4% (−13 c/1k here) and ADR-0002's 10% (-1,004 c/1k) worlds, and the λ_to price needs re-derivation.
- If quiet improvements at the starved-share scale become routine (e.g. acquirers onboarding/offboarding frequently), re-measure C2's reset-vs-floor decomposition on the starved variant before touching R49's η or γ — in the measured case the reset, not the floor, carried the refeed.
- If DECISION_LOG v1's provenance shows replay mismatches in production (any seq where re-derived != logged chain), the key-addressed-draws contract is broken — treat as an incident, never patch the log.
