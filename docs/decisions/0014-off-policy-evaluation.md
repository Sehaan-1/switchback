## Decision

1. **The estimator is clipped head-level IPS — weight w̃ = min(w, 10) — over exact-recomputed propensities, with a support gate in front of it and support diagnostics next to every number.** For every logged decision the DECISION_LOG v1 record (R60) already contains everything the recompute needs: eligible set, per-eligible-arm posterior snapshot, floor state, played chain. The propensity π₀(B|x) = P(B's score is the argmax, floor mixture included) is **recomputed by midpoint-quantile quadrature over the logged posterior** (24×4 nodes; sign-safe on negative `win+fee`), not read from the logged tag. Against a score-based MC-100k reference on 24 logged states: 0.09 pts chosen-arm mean |Δp|, 0.71 pts any-arm max, deterministic (no stream key moves it), 50 ms per full state. The alternatives measured: theta-only plug-in 61.2 pts (broken on this margin-skewed fleet), the R61 tag 14.0 pts — of which most is one sign trap (24.8% of decisions carry an eligible arm with `win+fee<0`, which silently reverses the score inequality), sign-fixed 5.7 pts, MC-64 1.8 pts [O1](2). A signed calibration probe confirms the recompute in aggregate: expected foxtrot-picks from the recomputed π₀ = 4,853 vs 4,785 observed head rows (ratio 1.014) [O5]. The shift family's weight is closed-form: w = 1−ρ on every row whose head is not B, (1−ρ)+ρ/π₀(B|x) on head-B rows only — the **mixture identity** that ties query cost to the target's head share, not the window [O1](3). **Clipping is not optional**: unclipped 1/π₀ rows are one absurd-π₀ log row away from fantasy numbers — +9,100,182 ¢/1k of error on one of the four validation worlds where a head row carried π₀≈1e-9 — while the clipped row on the same world errs by −836 ¢/1k [O5]. Self-normalization is printed alongside (clipped SNIPS), not shipped: it fixes one pathology (rescues the +9.1M row, dividing it back into the single-digit thousands) and buys a worse one (a stable +3,395 ¢/1k bias on the headline query, because the realized Ē[w] sits below 1 wherever the small-π₀ mass never fired).
2. **Doubly Robust is rejected for v1.** The case DR exists for — an untrustworthy propensity to insure against — this architecture never occupies: the propensity is recomputed exactly (calibration ratio 1.014, chooser replay bit-exact 1301/1301) [O1, O5]. The DR negative control prices the other universe: with the R61 tag as the weight, DR recovers from the tag's error better than IPS-on-tag (+917 vs −1,440 ¢/1k at ρ=0.20) — precisely the insurance DR sells, and precisely why the tag must never be the weight [O4]. Across four re-seeded worlds on the headline query, unclipped DR inherits IPS's tail catastrophe (+1,265,383 ¢/1k in the worst world), and the self-normalized-clipped DR (SNDR clip10) lands +1,096±283 — statistically no better than clipped IPS's −949±70, while paying an ~90 s-per-query decision-QMC for its mixture term that plain IPS does not need [O5].
3. **The counterfactual interface is `shift(target: processor, share: ρ∈(0,1], window: [t0,t1))` evaluated as an overlay on the logged trajectory.** Per decision, an independent key-addressed ρ-coin forces `target` to chain head when the constraint filter makes it eligible; the logged chain's remainder (minus target) is the fallback tail (depth ≤2 as shipped). Answer: `{value_c_1k, auth_rate, delta_c_1k, ci95, support:{head_rows, ess_frac, ew_bar}, verdict}` — CI by 400-rep decision bootstrap; verdict carries the §4 gate outcome, including **refusal**. The frozen-trajectory overlay is the panel's estimand: what a log can identify. The two semantic edges of that claim are measured, not assumed: the re-learning composite ("the fleet that lived the shift") diverges from the overlay by −9.8 ¢/1k over the 7-day window here [O2](3) and is ADR-0013's canary's question; the single in-weight approximation (fallback-tail draw conditioning) leaks +0.04 ¢/dec → +7.9 ¢/1k at ρ=0.20 [O2](2), two orders inside the §4 bound.
4. **The validation protocol is same-world replay with a measured floor; the acceptance gate is absolute-plus-relative with support diagnostics.** (V1) one content-addressed world serves both policies; (V2) run the logging policy once — the emitted DECISION_LOG is the input; (V3) replay the overlay through the frozen trajectory for ground truth (production equivalent: the ADR-0013 1–5% canary); (V4) report |estimate−truth| in ¢/1k, and vs |Δ| only when the effect is material; (V5) the protocol's floor is measured on re-seeded worlds, with every contending estimator priced on every world. Measured floor (headline query, 4×60k worlds): replayed Δ stable at −1,729.7±42.7 ¢/1k — the truth is stable; the per-world estimator table below attributes error to estimators. **Gate: PASS iff (a) ≥1,000 logged head-B rows in the window (else REFUSED, decided before any estimator runs) and (b) at N≥30,000 in-window decisions, |estimate−truth| ≤ max(1,500 ¢/1k, 75% of |Δ|)** — sized to hold the measured cluster (IPS-clip10 RMS 951 ¢/1k on the worst query class; ≤160 ¢/1k on support-rich classes) with headroom. Zero-support queries are refused, not extrapolated [O3, O5]. Any change to the estimator, quadrature, support thresholds, or the DECISION_LOG schema re-runs this gate; the guardrail is reproducibility of the verdict, not a one-time certification.
5. **Implementation: Python in `analysis/ope/` — library + thin CLI (`ope shift --target B --share 0.2 --window 7d`), stdlib-only; pandas/scipy optional accelerators, never correctness-critical.** Reads sealed trace partitions via a read replica (R94) or the ADR-0012 columnar export — never the hot path. Measured at the 60k window on this box: window scan 24 ms (2.5M rows/s, stdlib sqlite3), quadrature recompute 809 decisions/s; the recompute is the bottleneck by ~4 orders of magnitude [O6]. Therefore (a) per-target π₀ tables are computed **at partition-seal time** for the registered query vocabulary (one quadrature pass serves the whole ρ grid — weights are arithmetic in ρ once π₀ is known), so a pane refresh is a sum; (b) a cold 7-day query costs ≈43 s for an 8%-share target, ≈383 s for a 45%-share target, single core [O1](3). Fleet-scale arithmetic (labelled model): 7d @ 5,000 dps = 3.0e9 decisions; an 8%-share target ⇒ ~2e8 recomputes — single-core days avoided exactly by (a) [O6].

## Context

The ticket asks five questions — IPS mechanics and clipping at payment volumes; IPS vs DR; confirming the propensity-logging schema; the query interface and verification against replayed truth with an acceptable bound; implementation home and read path. Three frames decide how they resolve here:

- **The logging policy is adaptive, contextual, and margin-skewed — generic OPE lore does not port.** TS selects by the ADR-0007 score; the constraint filter routinely passes spread-positive-but-fixed-fee-underwater arms (24.8% of decisions here) — so propensities are resolution-hungry and sign-sensitive, and small-share targets carry π₀ mass down in 1e-3…1e-9 territory. The support story above ESS: across the four 60k worlds, the sum of 1/π₀ over logged head-foxtrot rows covers only 13.4k of the 41.6k eligible-row mass — 68% of the counterfactual mass sits in contexts the log never head-visited — and simultaneously, two worlds contain a single head row with π₀ ~ 1e-8, large enough to detonate unclipped estimates (+9.1M ¢/1k) [O5]. Both phenomena are the same missing-mass mechanism in opposite signs; only clipped weights keep money units.
- **DECISION_LOG v1 is sufficient; the dependency to confirm is behavioral, not structural.** [O1](1) re-runs C4's replay gate on this spike's run: 1301/1301 bit-exact. v1 already versions runs by header hashes, which the panel's support table keys on. What v1 does NOT settle is who may consume the logged tag: the measurements (14 pts of chosen-arm error; the [O4] negative control) force a hard rule, R107.
- **The honest uncertainty posture for an adaptive-policy window.** A decision-bootstrap CI is *correct for the estimator's sampling spread* and *silent about the missing-mass term* — observed: truth 25,625 lies outside the SNIPS-clip10 95% CI [28,465, 29,536] whose width is 269 ¢/1k [O5]. The panel therefore ships point + CI + support verdict (head rows, ESS/N, Ē[w]) as one artifact; the absolute-plus-relative gate is what the validation protocol certifies. External anchor (published benchmark, not measured here): on the Open Bandit Dataset demo (Saito et al., NeurIPS 2021; logged-bandit fashion-recommendation data, Bernoulli-TS target, universal behavior policy known) IPW/DR relative estimation errors land ≈8–14% of policy value and DM ≈21–23% — a known-behavior-propensity regime, which is exactly the regime this architecture sits in after the recompute swap. Here the achieved IPS-clip10 error on the headline query is ~3.7% of *policy value* (952/25,625) — inside that band and below DM-there — though 54% of the small |Δ| the PM actually asks about; the gate quotes both denominators.

### What was measured (spike 0015, six sections)

[O1] replay gate + propensity bake-off vs MC-100k + the mixture identity and its costs. [O2] the query semantics: the confounding priced in the open (foxtrot realizes 92.1 ¢/dec on its logged heads, 22.3 ¢/dec forced over all eligible contexts — the −69.9 ¢/dec the weights exist to remove), the tail-conditioning leak (+0.04 ¢/dec), the re-learning divergence (−9.8 ¢/1k over the window). [O3] per-grid estimator errors vs paired replayed truth, incl. the zero-support refusal case. [O4] DM/DR/SNDR under the strongest honest DM (engine posteriors → ADR-0002 margin + logged fallback term; on-support calibration within −314 ¢/1k) and the R61-tag-as-weight negative control. [O5] the validation protocol, its four-world floor, per-world estimator standings, the calibration (1.014) and Ē[w]/missing-mass accounting probes, a clip sweep, bootstrap CIs, and the proposed gate. [O6] the read path at 60k-row scale + the fleet-scale amortization model.

## Consideration-by-consideration analysis

### 1. IPS at payment volumes — mechanics, clipping, support

**Form.** Recommendation set = eligible set + played chain; the chain head is the unit of reweighting and the logged tail is reused per-decision — an approximation whose only content is whose draws chose the tail, measured ≤0.1 ¢/dec [O2](2). For `shift(B,ρ)` the mixture identity gives w exactly: 1−ρ off-target-head, (1−ρ)+ρ/π₀(B|x) on head-B rows, 1.0 where B is ineligible [O1](3).

**The measured stakes of clipping and normalization.** The four-world replicate table (foxtrot, ρ=0.20, N=60k; err ¢/1k vs each world's replayed truth; floor sd 42.7):

| **IPS clip10** | **−952.6** | **−836.4** | **−1,008.0** | **−997.2** | **−948.6** | **951.0** |

- Ē[w] per world: 0.869, 463.120, 46.635, 0.865 — heavy-tailed by design; both signs of missing mass appear. [O5]
- **Only the clipped rows keep money units.** IPS-clip10's error is a *stable, signed* −949±70: it deletes precisely the mass the log never sampled (68% of the counterfactual on this class). Clipped-SNDR buys nothing statistically distinguishable from that (1,127 RMS) and costs the QMC; SNIPS's divide rescues the exploding world (+9.1M→−5.4k) but its typical-case bias (+3.4k) is 3.5× the clipped-IPS figure. The **clip sweep** on the canonical world [O5] prices the cap choice directly: IPS error −1,652 / −1,177 / −953 / −864 / −943 ¢/1k for caps 1 / 3 / 10 / 30 / 100 (foxtrot ρ=0.20) and −237 / +46 / +50 / +57 / +64 on charlie — a shallow bowl around 3–100 with the canonical-world minimum near 30; 10 ships because (a) the sweep's spread within 3…100 is ≪ the cross-world catastrophic variation it exists to quell, (b) it is the only measured point optimal on *both* support regimes (charlie's is 3–10, foxtrot's is 10–30), and (c) the cross-world catastrophe rows (Ē[w]=463) are measured stable at 10 — four worlds, −836…−1,008 ¢/1k. The sweep is part of the re-graded gate (R110), so a future scenario re-prices the cap with evidence rather than taste.
- **Plain IPS beats SNIPS on every support-bearing row of the single-world grid** [O3]: e.g. charlie ρ=0.50: +163 vs +1,328; foxtrot ρ=0.05: −444 vs +456 — the realized Ē[w]<1 worlds make the self-normalizer amplify, and the panel should never see self-normalization without the Ē[w] diagnostic next to it.
- **Support-rich regime as designed control**: charlie (45% head share) errs by ≤+163 ¢/1k on all ρ at 60k — on near-no-op shifts (|Δ|<10 ¢/1k) the relative form is vacuous and the gate grades absolute error only.
- **Zero support**: delta (never headed in 7d) is REFUSED — the estimator rows show why: weights become the degenerate {1−ρ, 1} costume, `IPS≡IPS-clip10` at −106…−906, SNIPS +769…+7,681 — point answers from nothing [O3].

### 2. IPS or DR

DR buys consistency under *either* a good propensity model or a good reward model, plus control-variate variance reduction:

- **The propensity-leg premise does not exist here** (§1 calibration: 1.014; the replay gate bit-exact). The [O4] negative control measures the other world explicitly: tag-as-weight at ρ=0.20 puts IPS −1,440, SNIPS +2,915, DR +917 ¢/1k off-truth — DR indeed halves the damage, which is exactly the mechanism by which a *tag-consuming* panel would get itself into trouble; R107 forecloses that consumption.
- **The reward-model leg is honest but earns no keep.** The strongest possible DM (the fold's own posteriors → ADR-0002's two-part margin + the logged per-processor fallback contribution) is calibrated on-support within −322 ¢/1k [O4] and delivers +1,304 mean error on the headline query — 1.4× clipped-IPS's RMS — because the fallback term inherits the same context-selection the IPS weights were built to remove ([O2](1) prices the gap at −69.9 ¢/dec: an arm worth 92 ¢ where the policy likes it and 22 ¢ elsewhere).
- **Ablations priced**: naive table-DMs (head×ctx cell means) were tried first and fail catastrophically on an adaptive policy (+8,979…+27,484 ¢/1k at ρ=0.05…0.50, same protocol — the cell means inherit the logging policy's head selection where the shift extrapolates); they are documented in spike history as the reason the structural DM exists at all.
- External anchor: OBD's known-propensity benchmark puts DM ≈2× above IPW/DR on relative error; the ordering direction reproduces here.

**Verdict: clipped IPS headline, clipped SNIPS printed, DR/SNDR/DM not in v1.** Revisit only if logs stop carrying the posterior snapshot (the estimated-propensity regime = DR's premise) — §Reopen trigger (iii).

### 3. The schema dependency — confirmed, one rule hardened

DECISION_LOG v1 per decision: eligible arms, per-eligible-arm posterior snapshot (`alpha, beta, to_alpha, to_beta`), floor state, played chain, outcomes [R60]. Exercised end-to-end [O1]: replay 1301/1301 bit-exact; quadrature vs MC-100k at 0.09 pts; all sections' estimates downstream. **No schema change.** The floor is part of the propensity: π₀(B|x) = (1−η)·P(score-argmax) + η·1[B∈cold]/|cold|, exact because v1 logs the floor state. What changes is consumer law: **R107** — the R61 tag is provenance/debug only, never a weight (its 14-pt error and sign trap are [O1](2) measurements, and [O4]'s negative control prices what trusting it as a weight costs downstream).

### 4. The query interface and the verification protocol — with the achieved numbers

**Interface** (the ticket's ask, pinned): `shift(target: B, share: ρ∈(0,1], window: [t0,t1), tz=UTC)` → `{value_c_1k, auth_rate, delta_c_1k, ci95, support:{head_rows, ess_frac, ew_bar}, verdict ∈ {PASS, FAIL, REFUSED}}`. The panel's copy states the overlay estimand ("had we forced 20% onto B *in this window*, holding everything the log froze") and defers the re-learning composite to ADR-0013's canary — measured divergence −9.8 ¢/1k over the 7-day window [O2].

**Protocol**: V1–V5 per Decision 4. **Gate**: support first (≥1,000 head-B rows in window — decided before any estimator runs), then at N≥30k: |estimate−truth| ≤ max(1,500 ¢/1k, 75% of |Δ|); |Δ|<100 ¢/1k ⇒ absolute-only. Sizing, from the achieved table at the headline estimator (IPS-clip10) [O3, O5]: foxtrot-class −226.7 (ρ=0.05), −952.6 (ρ=0.20), −2,729.2 (ρ=0.50 — 62.5% of |Δ|, inside the 75% allowance); charlie-class +14.8/+50.2/+118.3; and the multi-world RMS 951.0. A candidate SNIPS-clip10 fails the same gate (+3,390 vs bound ≈1,500) — the gate discriminates estimators exactly as intended, rather than blessing whatever PASSED once. Bootstrap CIs are reported as *sampling statements about the clipped-IPS functional* (95% CI [24,185.3, 25,131.7], sd 237.5, width 946 ¢/1k on the headline query) next to the support diagnostics that price the missing-mass term; the CI is never presented as posterior coverage of the replayed truth — measured: the truth (25,624.7) lies outside it, by roughly 2 bootstrap-sd, consistent with the signed missing-mass term [O5].

**Answer to the ticket's "typical error rates in comparable domains"**: the published anchor is the OBD benchmark (IPW/DR ≈8–14% *of policy value*, DM ≈21–23%, behavior propensity known; Saito et al., NeurIPS 2021, cited qualitatively) — here the achieved 3.7% of value (54% of |Δ|) on the small-support class and ≤0.6% of value on the rich-support class is at-or-better-than that band, with the caveat that % of |Δ| is the denominator the PM actually asks about and it is intrinsically larger (small-signal regime).

### 5. Implementation

`analysis/ope/` per ADR-0011: `weights.py` (sign-safe 24×4-quadrature, constants pinned next to its tests), `estimators.py` (IPS/SNIPS ± clip, DM, DR, SNDR; ESS/Ē[w] diagnostics), `queries.py` (shift overlay + mixture identity; the π₀-table cache keyed by (target, partition)), `cli.py` (`ope shift --target B --share 0.2 --window 7d [--json]`), and the seal-time hook that registrable-query-vocabulary π₀ tables are built with. Reads: sealed partitions on a replica per R94 (ADR-0012), the columnar export when built. Measured: scan 2.0M rows/s; quadrature 586 decisions/s; end-to-end 7-day query ≈49 s (8%-share target) — comfortably inside refresh-in-minutes with the seal-time cache; the fleet-scale amortized model per [O6].

## Consequences

- The panel can answer the ticket's question ("+20% to processor B over 7 days?") in ~49 s at 7-day scale on a warm seal-time cache (~408 s worst-class, single core, stdlib) [O1](3), with a verdict the org can defend.
- Accepted costs, named: (a) clipped IPS carries a *signed* missing-mass bias on small-share targets (−949 ¢/1k stable across four worlds on the 8%-share class) — accepted because it is stable, signed, inside the gate, and the unbiased-priced-by-catastrophe alternatives are strictly worse on the same evidence; the panel shows the diagnostics it is inferred from rather than hiding them. (b) ρ=0.50-class queries on small-share targets sit near the gate's relative allowance (62.5% of |Δ| at 60k, ESS/N 0.005 — within budget but warm; a 30-day window re-certification is the follow-up, owned by #17). (c) The overlay estimand cannot price re-learning (−9.8 ¢/1k measured divergence here) — the panel's copy must say so; ADR-0013's canary owns the composite. (d) The seal-time π₀-table job is new scheduled work per (target, partition); 26.8k recomputes ≈ 46 s/day/shard for the richest class at this scenario's share — budgeted against (b) of ADR-0012's partitioning. (e) The cap constant (10) is pinned by the [O5] catastrophe rows and the sweep, not by theory; the sweep is part of the gate so a future scenario can re-price it.
- Rules added (sequence continues from R106):
  - **R107** — An OPE weight is always an exact recompute from the DECISION_LOG v1 record (sign-safe midpoint-quantile quadrature, 24×4 resolution, floor mixture included); the R61 logged tag is provenance and is never a weight. Introducing an estimated-propensity path requires a new ADR.
  - **R108** — Every counterfactual answer carries the §4 verdict; the support gate (≥1,000 head-B rows in window) runs before any estimator; a refused query is reported refused, never extrapolated.
  - **R109** — OPE reads sealed snapshot partitions (or the ADR-0012 columnar export) via a read replica only; nothing in `analysis/ope/` touches the hot write path.
  - **R110** — The [O5] replicate gate re-runs on any change to the estimator, quadrature constants, clip cap, support thresholds, or the DECISION_LOG schema; the thresholds live in the benchmark, not in comments.
  - **R111** — All OPE-side randomness (the ρ-coin, QMC reps, bootstraps) draws from the dedicated `ope` key space, disjoint from policy and world keys by construction.
- **Reopen triggers** (any one reopens): (i) the §4 replicate gate fails on the committed scenario (or a committed refresh of it); (ii) a committed scenario change moves the headline class's support below the gate in representative windows so the panel refuses its own headline question; (iii) a schema change drops the posterior snapshot (propensities become *estimated* — the DR premise materializes; [O4]'s negative control is the migration evidence); (iv) the floor policy changes (η, cold-set definition) without re-measuring the floor leg of π₀; (v) a live ADR-0013 deploy measures re-learning divergence > 500 ¢/1k over a comparable window — the overlay-as-estimand posture gets re-graded.
- **Alternatives considered and rejected**: full chain-level IPS (steelman: kills the tail-conditioning term entirely; rejected: tail-level propensities are products over active-set combinations — combinatorially less stable — and the measured head-level term is 0.04 ¢/dec, three orders below the gate's absolute floor); DR/SNDR as headline (steelman: insurance + variance; rejected: §2 — unclipped DR inherits the catastrophe, clipped SNDR is statistically indistinguishable from clipped IPS while paying a per-query QMC); DM-first for small windows (steelman: calibrated on-support; rejected: +8,979…+27,484 ¢/1k for naive cell means, +1,304 for even the structural DM on the headline class — its fallback term inherits exactly the context-selection gap the weights exist to remove); computing propensities engine-side at log time (steelman: amortizes to zero at query time; rejected: the hot path would pay for a cold-path query class; the recompute is already proven bit-exact and calibration-exact, and seal-time amortization gets the same economics without touching the write path).

## Notes on scope

All magnitudes are properties of `baseline-steady-v1@sha256:b49193b7e715` at the 7-day window with the shipped engine configuration (informative prior m=100, η=0.05, n_min=1000, floor_mode=onboard, draw_alg=exact, 2-attempt cap) — evidence for orderings, mechanisms, and gate numbers, not forecasts for a different fleet. The external OBD anchor is published benchmark data (Saito et al., NeurIPS 2021 Open Bandit Dataset/Pipeline demo), cited qualitatively (IPW/DR ≈8–14%, DM ≈21–23% relative error) as a comparable-domain cross-check; it is not a measurement made here.
